JarvisPrivacy policy

Private personal assistant

Jarvis helps one person get things done.

Jarvis is a private assistant operated by Sami Kayyal. It responds to the owner's WhatsApp messages and can use the owner's connected Google account to answer requests about Gmail, Drive, and Calendar.

Google access is granted by the owner. Gmail messages and Calendar events are changed only after the owner approves the exact action. Drive access is read-only. Jarvis is not open for public sign-up.
